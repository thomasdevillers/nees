Good to know 09 — Frequently asked

Questions,
answered.

Need something more specific? Send the details on WhatsApp and we’ll work through the setup with you.

Can I buy or rent the screens?+

Yes. Nees offers permanent purchases as well as rentals shaped around the duration, quantity and location of your project.

What is included with a rental?+

Screen rental, delivery and collection, setup and testing, loading your content, and basic remote or telephone support are included.

Can Nees prepare the content?+

We can load supplied content. Design or content conversion can also be added to your quote as an optional service.

How many screens can be linked?+

Up to six Nees Fold 2 screens can combine into one continuous display face.

Are the screens for indoor or outdoor use?+

The Nees Fold 2 is an IP45 indoor display intended for venues, events and branded indoor environments.

How is content loaded and controlled?+

Content can be controlled through the internet, an app or USB. Nees can load your supplied content as part of a rental setup.

How is pricing calculated?+

Quotes are based on rental duration or purchase requirements, screen quantity, delivery location and any content support needed.
